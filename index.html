I'm an incoming M.S. Data Science student at UC San Diego, where I also earned my B.S. in Cognitive Science (Specialization in Machine Learning). I'm originally from the San Francisco Bay Area.

I've worked with data in the healthcare industry at Kaiser Permanente and in the airline industry at ATPCO, leading A/B test experimentation, telling stories through effective reporting, and building machine learning solutions that informed product, marketing, and operations decisions.

I care deeply about accessibility and building AI systems that are safe, transparent, and human-centered. I contributed to an ACM FAccT paper with the Ujima Security & Privacy Research Group on accessibility gaps in human-like LLMs, and I'm part of the AI Alignment Club at UCSD, a community connecting students with opportunities in the AI Safety space.

Outside of work and research, I also love teaching! I've been a Peer Learning Assistant for COGS 9 (Introduction to Data Science) and helped build OCCTIVE, an NSF-funded platform for learning CS fundamentals.
