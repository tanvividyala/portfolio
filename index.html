Hi, I'm Tanvi. I use data to decode
how and why people do the things they do.

I'm an incoming master's student at UC San Diego studying Data Science. Previously, I also graduated from UC San Diego with a Bachelor's in Cognitive Science with a Specialization in Machine Learning and Neural Computation.

In industry, I've worked with data at organizations like Kaiser Permanente, one of the nation's largest healthcare systems, and ATPCO, the airline industry's leading source for merchandising and pricing data. There I've built dashboards, ML models, LLM extraction pipelines, and A/B test experiments that shape decisions across product, marketing, and operations teams.

I'm excited about accessibility in tech and building systems that are safe, transparent, and human-centered. I've contributed to an ACM FAccT paper with the Ujima Security & Privacy Research Group investigating accessibility gaps in human-like LLMs. I'm also part of the AI Alignment Club at UCSD, a community dedicated to raising awareness around AI Safety and connecting students with opportunities in that domain.

I also love teaching and am deeply interested in computing education. I've served as a Peer Learning Assistant for COGS 9 (Introduction to Data Science), building interactive tools and leading sessions, and worked with the UCSD Computer Science and Engineering Department to build OCCTIVE, an NSF-funded web platform hosting learning material on CS fundamentals.
